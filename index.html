Background

Click a protected area, link, pinch point or barrier for details. In Present, clicking a protected area shows the areas reachable along the network within 1 hop (orange) and 5 hops (light orange); press Esc to clear. Protected areas can be shown as dots (size = area) or as their real shapes: the protected land of each climate type, exactly as it was used in the model (overlapping or touching sites merged, parts smaller than 1 km² left out). Corridor current: Linkage Mapper style, class 1 (low, blue) to 5 (high, red), natural breaks. Groups: amphibians & reptiles 5 km, mammals 50 km, birds 100 km dispersal; 1 event = one dispersal distance, 5 events = five (gap to the analog area). All combined = mean class of the four groups (amphibians and reptiles both count; no corridor = class 1). 23,391 protected-area nodes (WDPA + Natura 2000, ≥ 1 km², per climate type); future climate 2061–2080, mean of 4 CMIP6 models. F. Rodenburg, University of Groningen, 2026.
